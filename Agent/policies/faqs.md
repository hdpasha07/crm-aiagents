# Enterprise Customer Support: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## 1. Order Cancellation & Modification
- **How do I cancel my order?** Orders can be cancelled instantly with a single click via your Customer Account Portal within **60 minutes** of order placement. After 60 minutes, orders enter warehouse automation and cannot be cancelled before dispatch; you may return the item following our standard 14-day return procedure.
- **Can I modify items in my order?** We cannot alter items or item quantities once an order is placed. Please cancel the order within the 60-minute cancellation window and place a new order.

## 2. Accepted Payment Options
- We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.
- We do NOT accept cash on delivery (COD), money orders, personal checks, or cryptocurrencies.

## 3. Subscription Management & Billing
- **How do I manage or cancel a recurring subscription?** You can pause, skip, or cancel your subscription at any time under **Account Settings > Manage Subscriptions**.
- **Deadline for changes**: Modifications or cancellations must be completed at least **48 hours prior** to the scheduled billing renewal cycle.

## 4. Account Security & Two-Factor Authentication
- **What should I do if I suspect unauthorized account access?** Immediately perform a password reset using the "Forgot Password" link and contact security@enterprise.com. 
- Multi-factor authentication (2FA via SMS or Authenticator App) is available and recommended for all accounts under Security Settings.

## 5. Support Response Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Standard customer support inquiries: Response guaranteed within **24 hours**.
- High-priority / Escalated tickets (double billing, fraud suspicion, legal notices): Response from a senior human tier agent guaranteed within **2 hours**.
