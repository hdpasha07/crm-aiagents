# Enterprise Customer Support: Shipping & Delivery Policy

## 1. Shipping Tiers & Delivery Windows
We offer three distinct shipping tiers for domestic continental deliveries:
- **Standard Shipping**: Delivery within **3 to 5 business days**. Costs $4.99, or **FREE** on all orders with a subtotal over $50.00.
- **Expedited 2-Day Shipping**: Guaranteed delivery within **2 business days**. Flat rate of $9.99.
- **Overnight Express Delivery**: Next business day delivery for orders placed prior to **1:00 PM EST** Monday through Thursday. Flat rate of $19.99.

## 2. Order Processing & Handling Time
- All standard warehouse orders are processed and packaged within **24 hours** (Monday through Friday, excluding national holidays).
- Orders submitted after 1:00 PM EST on Friday will be dispatched the following Monday morning.

## 3. Shipment Tracking & Notifications
- Automated shipment confirmation emails containing carrier tracking numbers (FedEx, UPS, USPS) are dispatched within **2 to 4 hours** of parcel departure from our facility.
- Real-time carrier tracking links are also accessible via the customer account order history portal.

## 4. Damaged or Lost Shipments
- **Damaged in Transit**: Must be reported to customer support within **48 hours** of carrier delivery scan, accompanied by photographic documentation of outer packaging and item damage. Immediate complimentary replacement will be dispatched.
- **Lost in Transit**: If a tracking status indicates no movement for **7 consecutive business days**, the package is formally designated as lost. Support will immediately issue a full refund or express replacement upon customer request.

## 5. Shipping Address Corrections
- Shipping addresses can be adjusted free of charge within **60 minutes** of order submission.
- Once an order changes to "Dispatched" or "Fulfilled", address changes cannot be executed by our system and the customer must contact the delivery carrier directly.
