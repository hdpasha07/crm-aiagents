# Enterprise Customer Support: Refund & Returns Policy

## 1. Return Window and Eligibility
- Customers may request a return within **14 calendar days** from the verified delivery date.
- Items must be in brand new, unused condition, with all original packaging, tags, manuals, and accessories intact.
- An original proof of purchase (Order ID or invoice number) is strictly required for all return and refund requests.

## 2. Non-Refundable Items
The following categories are non-refundable under all circumstances:
- Digital downloads, software license keys, and virtual gift cards.
- Intimate apparel and personal hygiene items once opened or seal is broken.
- Final sale, clearance, or custom-engraved merchandise.

## 3. Refund Processing Timelines & Methods
- Upon arrival at our fulfillment center, items undergo standard inspection within **1 to 2 business days**.
- Once approved, refunds are credited back to the **original payment method**.
- Bank and credit card processing typically takes **3 to 5 business days** to reflect on your statement.
- Under no circumstances can refunds be issued to an alternate payment method or third-party card.

## 4. Return Shipping Fees
- **Defective, Damaged, or Incorrect Items**: Free return shipping label provided. Customer will receive a 100% full refund or free replacement.
- **Change of Mind / Customer Preference Returns**: A standard **$5.99 return shipping and restocking fee** will be deducted from the final refund total.

## 5. Billing Errors & Unauthorized Charges
- In situations involving suspected duplicate payments, overcharges, or unauthorized billing, claims are assigned **High Priority** and routed immediately to senior billing specialists.
- Confirmed billing discrepancies are refunded within **24 hours**.
